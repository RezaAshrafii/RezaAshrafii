# Reza Ashrafi

### Statistics student at the University of Tehran · ML research and full-stack engineering

I work on machine-learning systems where the evaluation matters as much as the model: calibration, uncertainty, distribution shift, time-aware validation, and reproducible experiments. Alongside the research work, I build the software needed to make those ideas usable—APIs, local-first tools, interfaces, and clear review flows.

My background includes Electrical Engineering, current studies in Statistics at the University of Tehran, and software-development training at Maktab Sharif. I use AI tools heavily for implementation, testing, debugging, and documentation; the modelling choices, statistical assumptions, evaluation design, and final technical decisions remain mine.

## How I work

I am most interested in problems that sit between statistical reasoning and product engineering. That usually means defining the claim before running the experiment, keeping train/test boundaries honest, documenting what the data cannot support, and exposing uncertainty instead of hiding it behind a single score.

For applied work, I use Python, scikit-learn, FastAPI, TypeScript, React, Next.js, SQLite, Streamlit, GitHub Actions, and LLM/RAG tooling. The specific stack changes from project to project; reproducibility, transparent interfaces, and useful failure modes do not.

## Current direction

I am building toward reliable decision systems for sequential and structured data: calibrated prediction, shift-aware evaluation, and evidence-based intervention policies. The aim is to produce work that can be read, run, challenged, and improved—not just demonstrated.

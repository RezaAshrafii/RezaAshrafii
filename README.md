I build practical software at the intersection of full-stack engineering, statistical machine learning and AI systems. My background combines Electrical Engineering, current Statistics studies at the University of Tehran, and professional software-development training at Maktab Sharif.

My technical focus is reliable machine learning: uncertainty quantification, calibration, structured distribution shift, evaluation design and reproducible experiments. I also build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and structured LLM workflows, with a longer-term research direction toward uncertainty-aware decision-making, offline policy evaluation and statistically grounded model adaptation.

I use modern AI development tools to accelerate implementation, testing, debugging and documentation, while retaining responsibility for system design, statistical assumptions, validation and the final engineering decisions.

## What I work with

- **Full-stack development:** Python, TypeScript, React, Next.js, FastAPI and REST APIs
- **Data and machine learning:** statistical analysis, feature engineering, scikit-learn and evaluation design
- **AI systems:** LLM APIs, RAG pipelines, structured outputs, evaluation workflows and parameter-efficient adaptation concepts
- **Product engineering:** SQLite, local-first applications, responsive interfaces, testing and deployment

I care about clear system boundaries, leakage-safe evaluation, reproducibility, evidence-grounded output and software that remains useful beyond a demo.

## Current direction

I am developing a research-grade portfolio around predictive reliability under structured distribution shift. The next flagship direction is group-aware conformal evaluation for sequential prediction and decision risk, with statistical reporting, reproducible artifacts and an interactive engineering interface. The goal is to connect rigorous statistical reasoning with software that can be tested, reproduced and explained.
